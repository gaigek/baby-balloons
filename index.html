Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Baby Balloons does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or personal details are ever requested.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • The only things saved are the game settings a parent chooses, such as balloon speed or whether the voice is on. They stay on the device and are deleted when the app is deleted.
  • The optional voice is produced on the device by iOS's built-in voices. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to view, export, or delete. Baby Balloons is designed for young children and complies with Apple's Kids category requirements and children's privacy laws such as COPPA, because no data is collected from anyone.

Support

How do I open the grown-up settings?

Touch and hold the small lock button at the top of the game for 3 seconds, until the ring fills.

How do I keep my child from leaving the game?

iPhone and iPad let only a parent lock a game, using Apple's Guided Access:

  1. Open the Settings app → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on, and set a passcode under Passcode Settings.
  2. Open Baby Balloons, triple-click the side button (or Home button), and tap Start.
  3. To leave, triple-click again and enter your passcode, or double-click if you use Face ID.

Can I change the speed or size of the balloons?

Yes. In the grown-up settings you can choose slow, medium, or fast; big, bigger, or biggest; and how many balloons appear.
